Privacy Policy

Learn how Agrivaan values and protects your privacy and user data within our agricultural services ecosystem.

Our Commitment to Privacy

Agrivaan values the privacy of all users including farmers, machinery owners, and marketplace users. We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring transparency in how your data is handled.

Limited Information We Collect

To provide reliable agriculture and booking services, we collect limited personal information including:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Address/location
  • KYC documents
  • Bank details
  • Booking information
  • Payment transaction details

How We Use Your Information

This information is used strictly and only for the following operational purposes:

Account Verification Confirming user identity to maintain trust and credibility.
Booking Management Connecting and facilitating bookings between farmers and service owners.
Payment Processing Handling securely structured client transacting models.
Platform Security Enhancing customer support and ensuring robust, safe services.

Important Disclosures

Data Privacy: Agrivaan does not sell user data to third parties.

Transaction Security: Payment transactions are securely processed using trusted payment gateways including Razorpay.

Account Responsibility: Users are responsible for maintaining account confidentiality.

Location Services: Agrivaan may use location information for nearby machinery discovery, booking tracking, and operational services.

By using the Agrivaan application or website, users agree to this privacy policy.
