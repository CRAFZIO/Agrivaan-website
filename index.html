ABOUT AGRIVAAN

About AGRIVAAN: Revolutionizing Indian Agriculture Digitally

AGRIVAAN is an all-in-one agriculture services platform that connects farmers with machinery, seeds, fertilizers, logistics, financial support, skilled workers, and modern farming solutions in one ecosystem.

We operate in both B2B and B2C models, work as a sales and service partner for multiple companies, support agricultural exports, and help young farmers through training and skill development programs.

Our Mission

“To empower farmers with smart, affordable, and reliable agricultural services through one integrated platform — connecting machinery, inputs, labor, logistics, finance, and market opportunities to improve productivity, profitability, and rural livelihoods.”

Our Vision

“To build India’s most trusted agri-service ecosystem that transforms farming through technology, innovation, and sustainable growth — creating better opportunities for farmers, rural entrepreneurs, and agricultural businesses.”
